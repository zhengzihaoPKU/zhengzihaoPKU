# About Me
I'm a Ph.D student from School of Computer Science, Peking University. My research interest includes Physical Intelligence, AI system, System Design Automation (SDA) and Computer Architecture. I am very fortunate to be advised by CAS Member Prof. Hong Mei and Prof. Xiang Chen from School of Computer Science, Peking University.

I completed my master's degree at MemLab, Peking University, with my research focusing on in-memory computing and computer architecture. I was fortunate to receive guidance from Prof. Yimao Cai and Prof. Ling Liang.

# Research
My research interests lie at the intersection of Physical Intelligence, AI Systems, and System Design Automation — spanning from fundamental model architectures to large-scale deployment and automated optimization.
